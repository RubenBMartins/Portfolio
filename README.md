# Portfolio;

This is my **portfolio**, a website in which I will submit projects of mine, and introduce myself, as well as how I got here!

# Link to the website;
https://rubenbmartins.github.io/Portfolio/

# About the project;
The portfolio was built with a combination of **HTML**, **CSS**, and **JavaScript**, with **individual HTML pages**, a **shared stylesheet**, and **JavaScript scripts** bringing interactivity to certain elements.
The goal of this project was to make an **online resume**, where anyone can get to know you by just looking at it; your **educational background**, **professional background**, your **projects**, your **passions**, ways to **reach out**, and get to know you by just the **looks** of the website alone!
As such, I aimed to make this project as personal as possible, with illustrations I've drawn to carry the intended feeling of the project to this level of ambition.

# What you will find in this portfolio;
You'll first notice the Presentation page, where I will introduce myself, my skills, personal interests, and store my resume.
You can then access three pages:

Background
> This page documents my educational, and professional background. Where I've studied, and where I've worked, for how long, and the specifc studies I was pursuing, or job position I had.

Projects
> This page documents my projects, both professional and personal. At the time of writing this, truth be told, development needs to be done, and further documentation needs to be available for them.

Contact
> This page documents my socials, and hosts a form for you to reach out to me via e-mail, from the website itself. Although, it's subject to change, and with time, perhaps more socials will be documented there.

# The constraints and issues;
For a website, there is one very important feature, that being **responsiveness**.
> Making a website responsive entails making sure it displays well no matter the screen resolution, which was no easy task. Past commits will show issues related to tables causing issues with responsiveness due to imposed icon sizes. Or issues with pseudo-window being partially outside of the screen, which in some contexts will still be true, as I preferred the contents of the window to stay visible, and not be squished into unreadable pixels.

Another constraint given was **time**, and overall **availability**.
> Projects are handled simulteanously, as such, it wouldn't be reasonable to focus on only one, to leave out the rest. As such, this project was running on a time limit, which I'd like to think I managed well enough to have good content in this Portfolio, despite that.
